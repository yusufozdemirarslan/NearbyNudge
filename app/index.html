See It In Action

Same prompt. Genuinely different emails.

Location: Arlington, VA  |  Industry: Dentists  |  Prompt: "We help local dental practices get more patients through targeted Google Ads. Offer a free 15-min audit. Professional tone."
⭐ High-Rated Practice
Bright Smiles Dental (4.9★, 280 reviews)
Subject: Your reviews are impressive — here's how to turn that into more patients
Hi Dr. [Name], I came across Bright Smiles Dental and your 4.9-star rating with 280 reviews is outstanding. Practices with that reputation see incredible ROI from targeted Google Ads because the trust is already built. I'd love to show you how we could turn that into 15–20 additional new patient bookings per month. Would a quick 15-minute audit call this week work?
👁️ Low-Visibility Practice
Arlington Family Dentistry (3.8★, 22 reviews)
Subject: A quick way to boost visibility for Arlington Family Dentistry
Hi Dr. [Name], I noticed Arlington Family Dentistry has a solid community presence but may not be fully visible to patients searching online. A short Google Ads campaign could help you appear right when local patients search for a new dentist. I'd be happy to run a free 15-minute audit. Would Tuesday or Wednesday work?
The AI adapts subject line, opening hook, value prop, and CTA based on each business's unique Google Maps signals.
