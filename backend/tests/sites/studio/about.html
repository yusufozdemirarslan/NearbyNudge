About Sample Dental Studio

Dr. Jordan Lee has served Arlington families since 2009. After dental school and a residency in general dentistry, Dr. Lee opened Sample Dental Studio to offer careful, unhurried care close to home.

Our practice is small on purpose. Most patients see the same hygienist at every visit, and we keep appointments long enough to answer questions without rushing.
