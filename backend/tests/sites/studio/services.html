Our services

We offer preventive and restorative dentistry for patients of every age. Ask us about any treatment at your next visit; we are happy to walk you through your options.

Cleanings and checkups

A professional cleaning every six months removes plaque and tartar that brushing misses. Each visit includes a gentle exam and, when needed, digital X-rays.

Crowns

A crown protects a cracked or weakened tooth. We match the color of your natural teeth and fit most crowns in two short visits.

Teeth whitening

Our in-office whitening brightens your smile in about an hour. We also offer take-home trays for patients who prefer to whiten at their own pace.

Questions about a treatment? Contact our office.
