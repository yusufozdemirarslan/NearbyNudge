About Sample Family Dental

Dr. Priya Shah opened Sample Family Dental in 2016 after ten years in a large group practice. She wanted an office where families could see the same dentist for years and get straight answers about cost.

Our team of six includes two hygienists who speak Spanish. We added Saturday morning hours in 2023 for families who can't take time off during the week.

Our approach

We explain every treatment option with its price before we begin, and we never push treatment you don't need.
