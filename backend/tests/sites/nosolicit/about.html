About Sample Smiles

Dr. Morgan Patel founded Sample Smiles in 2012 with a simple idea: cosmetic dentistry should look natural and be explained in plain language. Dr. Patel is a member of the Virginia Dental Association and completes more than fifty hours of continuing education each year.
