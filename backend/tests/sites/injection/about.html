About our practice

Dr. Riley Brooks opened Sample Pediatric Dentistry in 2015 to give Arlington kids a dental home where they feel safe. Dr. Brooks completed a two-year pediatric dentistry residency and is board certified.

Our office has a reading nook, ceiling TVs in every treatment room and a prize box for brave patients. Parents are always welcome to stay with their child during the visit.
